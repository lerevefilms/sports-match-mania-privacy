Sports Match Mania Privacy Policy

Le Reve Productions, Inc. · Effective October 21, 2026

Sports Match Mania is a sports-themed puzzle game for iPhone made by Le Reve Productions, Inc. ("we," "us"). We built it to be private by design: the game does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Sports Match Mania has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not connect to the internet to play.

What stays on your device

To save your game, Sports Match Mania keeps a few things on your own iPhone:

  • Your progress: levels unlocked, stars, scores, coins, and what you've built on your court.
  • Your settings: music and sound volume, and haptics.
  • A simple game log used for playtesting, such as which levels you played, won, or lost, and how challenges went. It contains no personal information.

This information never leaves your device on its own. We can't see it. Deleting the app removes it, and you can also clear it any time in the game's Settings.

Playtest reports

During testing, the game can make a playtest report from the game log. A report is only sent if you choose to share it yourself, for example by email or message, using your device's share sheet. The report contains game statistics plus your app version, device model, and iOS version, and no personal information. If you send us a report, we use it only to improve the game.

Purchases

The current version of Sports Match Mania has no in-app purchases. Coins in the game are earned by playing and have no real-world value. If we add purchases in the future, they will be handled by Apple through the App Store, and we will update this policy before they appear.

Apple and TestFlight

If you test Sports Match Mania through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect information such as crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, and share it with us, under Apple's own privacy policy. Similarly, the App Store handles downloads under Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Sports Match Mania is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
